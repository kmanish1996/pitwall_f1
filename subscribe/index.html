Get F1 2026 in your calendar

Every Grand Prix and every session of the 2026 season, in your own time zone. Free, no sign-up.

Subscribe once and every race weekend appears in your phone's calendar: practice, qualifying, sprints and the race. When a weekend's schedule is confirmed or changes, your calendar updates by itself. Each finished race shows the winner and a link to the replay.

Other calendar apps

Copy this address and add it as a calendar from a URL (sometimes called "Subscribe" or "From web"):

https://pitwallf1.in/f1-2026.ics

  • Android: add it with Google Calendar on a computer (Settings → Add calendar → From URL); it then syncs to your phone.
  • Outlook desktop: Add calendar → Subscribe from web, then paste the address.
  • Calendar apps check for changes every few hours. Times are stored in UTC and shown in your local time.
