Miami Grand Prix 2026: race replay & lap-by-lap story

Round 4 of 23 · Miami International Autodrome, Miami · 1–3 May 2026 · 57 laps

Kimi Antonelli won the 2026 Miami Grand Prix from pole position, 3.264s ahead of Lando Norris, with Oscar Piastri completing the podium.

▶ Watch the full race replayTiming tower, track map, race control messages and team radio, lap by lap.

How the race unfolded

The lead changed hands 5 times. Laps led: Kimi Antonelli (30 laps), Lando Norris (15 laps), Charles Leclerc (11 laps), Max Verstappen (1 lap).

The race was neutralised by a safety car on laps 6–11.

Kimi Antonelli made 1 pit stop (Medium → Hard). Finishers averaged 1.1 stops, while Lance Stroll came in 2 times.

Biggest climber: Gabriel Bortoleto, from P21 on the grid to P12 at the flag (+9).

4 drivers didn't finish: Nico Hülkenberg, Liam Lawson, Pierre Gasly, Isack Hadjar.

Fastest lap: Lando Norris with a 1:31.869.

Top speed through the speed trap: 334 km/h by George Russell.

Why the race went this way

The decisive move. Kimi Antonelli passed Max Verstappen on track for the lead on lap 29, and led the rest of the way.

Pit-stop battles. Kimi Antonelli undercut Lando Norris: in on lap 26, 1 lap earlier, and out in front.

Safety car timing. Max Verstappen pitted under the safety car on laps 6–11. Max Verstappen gained most: P8 before it, P5 at the flag. Kimi Antonelli stayed out.

Race pace. On clean laps, Kimi Antonelli's typical lap was 0.180s quicker than Lando Norris's.

Teammates. Kimi Antonelli P1 vs George Russell P4 (started P1 vs P5). Lando Norris P2 vs Oscar Piastri P3 (started P4 vs P7).
