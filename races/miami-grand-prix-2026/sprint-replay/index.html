Miami Grand Prix 2026: sprint replay & lap-by-lap story

Round 4 of 23 · Miami International Autodrome, Miami · 1–3 May 2026 · 19 laps

Lando Norris won the 2026 Miami Grand Prix sprint from pole position, 3.766s ahead of Oscar Piastri, with Charles Leclerc completing the podium.

Lando Norris led every one of the 19 laps.
