Chinese Grand Prix 2026: race replay & lap-by-lap story

Round 2 of 23 · Shanghai International Circuit, Shanghai · 13–15 Mar 2026 · 56 laps

Kimi Antonelli won the 2026 Chinese Grand Prix from pole position, 5.515s ahead of George Russell, with Lewis Hamilton completing the podium.

▶ Watch the full race replayTiming tower, track map, race control messages and team radio, lap by lap.

How the race unfolded

The lead changed hands 1 time. Laps led: Kimi Antonelli (55 laps), Lewis Hamilton (1 lap).

The race was neutralised by a safety car on laps 10–14.

Kimi Antonelli made 1 pit stop (Medium → Hard). Finishers averaged 1.1 stops, while Isack Hadjar came in 2 times.

Biggest climber: Carlos Sainz, from P17 on the grid to P9 at the flag (+8).

7 drivers didn't finish: Max Verstappen, Fernando Alonso, Lance Stroll, Oscar Piastri, Lando Norris, Gabriel Bortoleto, Alexander Albon.

Fastest lap: Kimi Antonelli with a 1:35.275.

Top speed through the speed trap: 353 km/h by Pierre Gasly.

Why the race went this way

The decisive move. Kimi Antonelli passed Lewis Hamilton on track for the lead on lap 2, and led the rest of the way.

Safety car timing. Under the safety car on laps 10–14, 7 of the eventual top ten pitted: Kimi Antonelli, George Russell, Lewis Hamilton, Charles Leclerc and 3 more. Isack Hadjar gained most: P18 before it, P8 at the flag.

Race pace. On clean laps George Russell was actually 0.068s a lap quicker than Kimi Antonelli, so track position won it.

Teammates. Kimi Antonelli P1 vs George Russell P2 (started P1 vs P2). Lewis Hamilton P3 vs Charles Leclerc P4 (started P3 vs P4).
