Austrian Grand Prix 2026: race replay & lap-by-lap story

Round 8 of 23 · Red Bull Ring, Spielberg · 26–28 Jun 2026 · 71 laps

George Russell won the 2026 Austrian Grand Prix from pole position, 1.611s ahead of Max Verstappen, with Kimi Antonelli completing the podium.

How the race unfolded

The lead changed hands 5 times. Laps led: George Russell (57 laps), Kimi Antonelli (8 laps), Max Verstappen (6 laps).

The race was neutralised by a virtual safety car on laps 25–26; a virtual safety car on laps 52–53.

George Russell made 2 pit stops (Medium → Hard). Finishers averaged 2.2 stops, while Lewis Hamilton came in 3 times.

Biggest climber: Oscar Piastri, from P7 on the grid to P4 at the flag (+3).

4 drivers didn't finish: Lance Stroll, Carlos Sainz, Sergio Pérez, Valtteri Bottas.
