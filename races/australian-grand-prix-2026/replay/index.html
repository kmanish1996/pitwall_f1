Australian Grand Prix 2026: race replay & lap-by-lap story

Round 1 of 23 · Albert Park, Melbourne · 6–8 Mar 2026 · 58 laps

George Russell won the 2026 Australian Grand Prix from pole position, 2.974s ahead of Kimi Antonelli, with Charles Leclerc completing the podium.

How the race unfolded

The lead changed hands 4 times. Laps led: George Russell (32 laps), Charles Leclerc (23 laps), Lewis Hamilton (3 laps).

The race was neutralised by a virtual safety car on laps 12–14; a virtual safety car on laps 18–20; a virtual safety car on lap 34.

George Russell made 1 pit stop (Medium → Hard). Finishers averaged 1.4 stops, while Carlos Sainz came in 3 times.

5 drivers didn't finish: Fernando Alonso, Valtteri Bottas, Isack Hadjar, Oscar Piastri, Nico Hülkenberg.
