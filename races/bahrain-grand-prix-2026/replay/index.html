Round 16 of 23 · Sepang International Circuit, Sepang, Malaysia · 2–4 Oct 2026 · 55 laps

How the race unfolded

The lead changed hands 5 times. Laps led: Max Verstappen (43 laps), Kimi Antonelli (11 laps), Oscar Piastri (1 lap).

The race was neutralised by a safety car on laps 9–12; a virtual safety car on laps 43–45; a safety car on laps 45–52.

Max Verstappen made 3 pit stops (Intermediate → Soft). Finishers averaged 3.5 stops, while Sergio Pérez came in 7 times.

3 drivers didn't finish: George Russell, Alexander Albon, Valtteri Bottas.
