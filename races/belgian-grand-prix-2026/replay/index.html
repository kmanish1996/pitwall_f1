How the race unfolded

The lead changed hands 4 times. Laps led: Kimi Antonelli (29 laps), Charles Leclerc (13 laps), Lando Norris (2 laps).

The race was neutralised by a safety car on laps 1–5; a virtual safety car on lap 18; a virtual safety car on laps 20–21.
