Canadian Grand Prix 2026: sprint replay & lap-by-lap story

Round 5 of 23 · Circuit Gilles-Villeneuve, Montréal · 22–24 May 2026 · 23 laps

George Russell won the 2026 Canadian Grand Prix sprint from pole position, 1.272s ahead of Lando Norris, with Kimi Antonelli completing the podium.
