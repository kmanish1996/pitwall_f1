Singapore Grand Prix 2026: race replay & lap-by-lap story

Round 17 of 23 · Marina Bay Street Circuit, Singapore · 9–11 Oct 2026 · 62 laps

Max Verstappen won the 2026 Singapore Grand Prix from pole position, 7.335s ahead of Charles Leclerc, with Kimi Antonelli completing the podium.

▶ Watch the full race replayTiming tower, track map, race control messages and team radio, lap by lap.

How the race unfolded

Max Verstappen led every one of the 62 laps.

The race was neutralised by a virtual safety car on laps 8–9; a virtual safety car on laps 39–40.

Max Verstappen made 1 pit stop (Soft → Medium). Finishers averaged 1.2 stops, while Sergio Pérez came in 2 times.

Biggest climber: Isack Hadjar, from P22 on the grid to P10 at the flag (+12).

4 drivers didn't finish: Alexander Albon, Lance Stroll, Franco Colapinto, Gabriel Bortoleto.

Fastest lap: Charles Leclerc with a 1:35.202.

Top speed through the speed trap: 308 km/h by George Russell.

Why the race went this way

Safety car timing. Under the virtual safety car on laps 39–40, 4 of the eventual top ten pitted: Charles Leclerc, Kimi Antonelli, Oscar Piastri and George Russell. Max Verstappen stayed out.

Race pace. On clean laps Charles Leclerc was actually 0.080s a lap quicker than Max Verstappen, so track position won it.

Teammates. Max Verstappen P1 vs Isack Hadjar P10 (started P1 vs P22). Charles Leclerc P2 vs Lewis Hamilton P6 (started P2 vs P3). Kimi Antonelli P3 vs George Russell P9 (started P4 vs P19).
