Singapore Grand Prix 2026: sprint replay & lap-by-lap story

Round 17 of 23 · Marina Bay Street Circuit, Singapore · 9–11 Oct 2026 · 20 laps

Max Verstappen won the 2026 Singapore Grand Prix sprint from pole position, 2.591s ahead of Lewis Hamilton, with Charles Leclerc completing the podium.

▶ Watch the full sprint replayTiming tower, track map, race control messages and team radio, lap by lap.

How the sprint unfolded

Max Verstappen led every one of the 20 laps.

The race was neutralised by a safety car on laps 2–6.

Max Verstappen didn't stop (Intermediate tyres throughout). No finisher made a stop.

Biggest climber: Carlos Sainz, from P22 on the grid to P12 at the flag (+10).

9 drivers didn't finish: Oscar Piastri, Alexander Albon, Gabriel Bortoleto, Valtteri Bottas, Isack Hadjar, Franco Colapinto, George Russell, Sergio Pérez, Lance Stroll.

Fastest lap: Lewis Hamilton with a 1:45.457.

Top speed through the speed trap: 277 km/h by Oscar Piastri.

Why the sprint went this way

Race pace. On clean laps, Max Verstappen's typical lap was 0.132s quicker than Lewis Hamilton's.

Teammates. Max Verstappen P1 vs Isack Hadjar retired (started P1 vs P9). Lewis Hamilton P2 vs Charles Leclerc P3 (started P6 vs P3).

Worked out from the lap times, pit-lane log and lap-by-lap order. Race pace is each driver's median lap, leaving out lap 1, in- and out-laps and safety car laps.
