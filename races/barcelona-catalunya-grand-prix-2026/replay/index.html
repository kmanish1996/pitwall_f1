Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix 2026: race replay & lap-by-lap story

Round 7 of 23 · Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya, Barcelona · 12–14 Jun 2026 · 66 laps

Lewis Hamilton won the 2026 Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix from P2 on the grid, 19.561s ahead of George Russell, with Lando Norris completing the podium.

▶ Watch the full race replayTiming tower, track map, race control messages and team radio, lap by lap.

How the race unfolded

The lead changed hands 5 times. Laps led: George Russell (32 laps), Lewis Hamilton (29 laps), Kimi Antonelli (4 laps), Charles Leclerc (1 lap).

The race was neutralised by a virtual safety car on laps 41–42; a virtual safety car on laps 63–65.

Lewis Hamilton made 3 pit stops (Soft → Hard → Medium → Hard). Finishers averaged 2.5 stops.

Biggest climber: Pierre Gasly, from P14 on the grid to P7 at the flag (+7).

7 drivers didn't finish: Charles Leclerc, Kimi Antonelli, Oliver Bearman, Fernando Alonso, Nico Hülkenberg, Valtteri Bottas, Lance Stroll.

Fastest lap: Lewis Hamilton with a 1:20.122.

Top speed through the speed trap: 358 km/h by Esteban Ocon.

Why the race went this way

The decisive move. Lewis Hamilton took the lead for good on lap 38, when Kimi Antonelli pitted. Lewis Hamilton stayed out until lap 41 and stopped under the safety car without losing the lead.

Pit-stop battles. Max Verstappen overcut Oscar Piastri: stayed out 4 laps longer, until lap 40, and rejoined ahead.

Safety car timing. Lewis Hamilton pitted under the virtual safety car on laps 41–42.

Race pace. On clean laps, Lewis Hamilton's typical lap was 0.858s quicker than George Russell's.

Teammates. Lewis Hamilton P1 vs Charles Leclerc retired (started P2 vs P10). George Russell P2 vs Kimi Antonelli retired (started P1 vs P3). Lando Norris P3 vs Oscar Piastri P5 (started P4 vs P7).
