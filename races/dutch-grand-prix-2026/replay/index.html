Dutch Grand Prix 2026: race replay & lap-by-lap story

Round 12 of 23 · Circuit Zandvoort, Zandvoort · 21–23 Aug 2026 · 72 laps

Lando Norris won the 2026 Dutch Grand Prix from pole position, 11.536s ahead of Kimi Antonelli, with George Russell completing the podium.

How the race unfolded

The lead changed hands 6 times. Laps led: Kimi Antonelli (32 laps), Lando Norris (31 laps), Lewis Hamilton (9 laps).

The race was neutralised by a virtual safety car on laps 55–57; a virtual safety car on lap 70; a red flag on lap 2.

Lando Norris made 2 pit stops (Medium → Soft → Hard). Finishers averaged 2.2 stops, while Franco Colapinto came in 3 times.

Biggest climber: Fernando Alonso, from P18 on the grid to P9 at the flag (+9).

6 drivers didn't finish: Alexander Albon, Valtteri Bottas, Esteban Ocon, Lance Stroll, Oliver Bearman, Max Verstappen.

Fastest lap: Charles Leclerc with a 1:14.230.

Top speed through the speed trap: 336 km/h by Arvid Lindblad.
