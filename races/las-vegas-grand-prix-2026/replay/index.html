Las Vegas Grand Prix 2026: race replay & lap-by-lap story

Round 21 of 23 · Las Vegas Strip Circuit, Las Vegas · 19–21 Nov 2026 · 50 laps

Max Verstappen won the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix from pole position, 0.639s ahead of Lando Norris, with Oscar Piastri completing the podium.

▶ Watch the full race replayTiming tower, track map, race control messages and team radio, lap by lap.

How the race unfolded

The lead changed hands 10 times. Laps led: Max Verstappen (44 laps), George Russell (3 laps), Kimi Antonelli (3 laps).

The race was neutralised by a safety car on laps 19–25.

Max Verstappen made 1 pit stop (Soft → Medium). Finishers averaged 1.2 stops, while Isack Hadjar came in 2 times.

Biggest climber: Oscar Piastri, from P17 on the grid to P3 at the flag (+14).

4 drivers didn't finish: Fernando Alonso, Arvid Lindblad, Sergio Pérez, Alexander Albon.

Fastest lap: Liam Lawson with a 1:35.591.

Top speed through the speed trap: 330 km/h by Charles Leclerc.

Why the race went this way

The decisive move. Max Verstappen passed Kimi Antonelli on track for the lead on lap 45, and led the rest of the way.

Safety car timing. Under the safety car on laps 19–25, 10 of the eventual top ten pitted: Max Verstappen, Lando Norris, Oscar Piastri, Kimi Antonelli and 6 more. Oscar Piastri gained most: P9 before it, P3 at the flag.

Race pace. On clean laps Lando Norris was actually 0.319s a lap quicker than Max Verstappen, so track position won it. The best race pace in the top ten belonged to Oscar Piastri, who finished P3.

Teammates. Max Verstappen P1 vs Isack Hadjar P14 (started P1 vs P4). Lando Norris P2 vs Oscar Piastri P3 (started P3 vs P17).
