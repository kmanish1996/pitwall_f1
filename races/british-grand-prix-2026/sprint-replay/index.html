British Grand Prix 2026: sprint replay & lap-by-lap story

Round 9 of 23 · Silverstone Circuit, Silverstone · 3–5 Jul 2026 · 17 laps

Kimi Antonelli won the 2026 British Grand Prix sprint from P2 on the grid, 2.745s ahead of Lewis Hamilton, with Lando Norris completing the podium.

How the sprint unfolded

The lead changed hands 1 time. Laps led: Kimi Antonelli (10 laps), Lewis Hamilton (7 laps).

Kimi Antonelli didn't stop (Medium tyres throughout). No finisher made a stop.

Biggest climber: Alexander Albon, from P22 on the grid to P18 at the flag (+4).

Fastest lap: Kimi Antonelli with a 1:31.607.

Top speed through the speed trap: 340 km/h by Isack Hadjar.

Why the sprint went this way

The decisive move. Kimi Antonelli passed Lewis Hamilton on track for the lead on lap 8, and led the rest of the way.

Race pace. On clean laps, Kimi Antonelli's typical lap was 0.088s quicker than Lewis Hamilton's.

Teammates. Kimi Antonelli P1 vs George Russell P4 (started P2 vs P5). Lewis Hamilton P2 vs Charles Leclerc P5 (started P1 vs P4). Lando Norris P3 vs Oscar Piastri P7 (started P6 vs P7).
