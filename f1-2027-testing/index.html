F1 2027 pre-season testing

Bahrain International Circuit, Sakhir · 24–27 Feb 2027 · live tracker

Formula 1's only pre-season test of 2027 runs over 4 days at Bahrain International Circuit, Sakhir, from Wednesday 24 February. This page tracks every day: fastest laps, tyres and the laps each team completes.

Before the cars run

The tracker fills in automatically after each day of running: the fastest lap of every driver, the tyre it was set on, and how many laps each team managed, which matters more than the lap times in testing.

How 2026 testing went

In 2026 the teams ran 6 days of testing in Bahrain. The fastest lap was 1:31.992 by Charles Leclerc (Ferrari), and McLaren did the most laps (775). Testing times are a weak guide: teams run different fuel loads, engine modes and tyres.
