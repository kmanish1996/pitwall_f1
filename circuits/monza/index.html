Autodromo Nazionale Monza

Monza · Round 13 of the 2026 season · Italian Grand Prix 2026

Autodromo Nazionale Monza is a 5.793 km Formula 1 circuit in Monza with 11 corners, first used for a world championship Grand Prix in 1950. Kimi Antonelli won the 2026 Italian Grand Prix here. Pierre Gasly's 2026 pole lap of 1:21.786 peaked at 342 km/h.

Interesting facts

  • The Temple of Speed. On the pole lap the car was at full throttle for 78% of the lap, the most of any track here.
  • More world championship Grands Prix than anywhere else. Monza has been on the calendar almost every year since 1950.
  • Built in 1922 inside the royal park of Monza. The old banked oval still stands next to the modern track.
  • Low downforce. Teams bring their slimmest wings here, and the pole lap reached 342 km/h.

Questions and answers

How long is Autodromo Nazionale Monza?

Autodromo Nazionale Monza is 5.793 km long, with 11 corners. The 2026 Italian Grand Prix runs over 53 laps, a race distance of 307.029 km.

What is the lap record at Autodromo Nazionale Monza?

The race lap record is 1:20.901, set by Lando Norris (McLaren) in 2025.

Who was on pole for the 2026 Italian Grand Prix?

Pierre Gasly (Alpine) took pole with a 1:21.786. On that lap the car reached 342 km/h and averaged 254 km/h.

Who won the 2026 Italian Grand Prix?

Kimi Antonelli won the 2026 Italian Grand Prix, in a race time of 1:51:15.281.

When was the first Formula 1 race at Autodromo Nazionale Monza?

1950.
