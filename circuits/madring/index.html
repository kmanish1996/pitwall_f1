Madring

Madrid · Round 14 of the 2026 season · Spanish Grand Prix 2026

Madring is a 5.414 km Formula 1 circuit in Madrid with 22 corners, first used for a world championship Grand Prix in 2026. Kimi Antonelli won the 2026 Spanish Grand Prix here. Lando Norris's 2026 pole lap of 1:31.824 peaked at 334 km/h.

Interesting facts

  • Brand new for 2026. Part street circuit, part purpose-built, it runs around the IFEMA exhibition centre in Madrid.
  • The Spanish Grand Prix comes to Madrid for the first time since 1981, when it was held at Jarama.
  • La Monumental. Its signature corner is a long, banked turn.
  • Lots of corners. With 22 turns, it has more corners than any other track on the 2026 calendar.

Questions and answers

How long is Madring?

Madring is 5.414 km long, with 22 corners. The 2026 Spanish Grand Prix runs over 57 laps, a race distance of 308.598 km.

What is the lap record at Madring?

The race lap record is 1:35.587, set by George Russell (Mercedes) in 2026.

Who was on pole for the 2026 Spanish Grand Prix?

Lando Norris (McLaren) took pole with a 1:31.824. On that lap the car reached 334 km/h and averaged 210 km/h.

Who won the 2026 Spanish Grand Prix?

Kimi Antonelli won the 2026 Spanish Grand Prix, in a race time of 1:34:23.754.
