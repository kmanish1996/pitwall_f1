Hungaroring

Mogyoród · Round 11 of the 2026 season · Hungarian Grand Prix 2026

Hungaroring is a 4.381 km Formula 1 circuit in Mogyoród with 14 corners, first used for a world championship Grand Prix in 1986. Lando Norris won the 2026 Hungarian Grand Prix here. Lando Norris's 2026 pole lap of 1:17.207 peaked at 331 km/h.

Interesting facts

  • Behind the Iron Curtain. In 1986 it hosted the first Formula 1 race in a communist country.
  • "Monaco without the walls". It's tight and twisty, so overtaking is hard and qualifying matters a lot.
  • Built in a valley. Fans on the hillsides can see much of the lap from one spot.
  • Busy on the pedals. The pole lap was at full throttle for only 46% of the lap, one of the lowest figures of the year.

Questions and answers

How long is Hungaroring?

Hungaroring is 4.381 km long, with 14 corners. The 2026 Hungarian Grand Prix runs over 70 laps, a race distance of 306.670 km.

What is the lap record at Hungaroring?

The race lap record is 1:16.627, set by Lewis Hamilton (Mercedes) in 2020.

Who was on pole for the 2026 Hungarian Grand Prix?

Lando Norris (McLaren) took pole with a 1:17.207. On that lap the car reached 331 km/h and averaged 202 km/h.

Who won the 2026 Hungarian Grand Prix?

Lando Norris won the 2026 Hungarian Grand Prix, in a race time of 1:39:56.180.

When was the first Formula 1 race at Hungaroring?

1986.
