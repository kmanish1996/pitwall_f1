Circuit of the Americas

Austin · Round 18 of the 2026 season · United States Grand Prix 2026

Circuit of the Americas is a 5.513 km Formula 1 circuit in Austin with 20 corners, first used for a world championship Grand Prix in 2012. Kimi Antonelli won the 2026 United States Grand Prix here. Kimi Antonelli's 2026 pole lap of 1:34.035 peaked at 341 km/h.

Interesting facts

  • A wall of a first corner. Turn 1 is a blind hairpin at the top of a steep climb from the start line.
  • Greatest hits. The fast esses in the first sector were inspired by famous corners elsewhere, such as Silverstone's Maggotts and Becketts.
  • F1 back in America. Opened in 2012, it gave the United States Grand Prix a permanent home.
  • Up and down. The pole lap covers 29.3 m from lowest to highest point.

Questions and answers

How long is Circuit of the Americas?

Circuit of the Americas is 5.513 km long, with 20 corners. The 2026 United States Grand Prix runs over 56 laps, a race distance of 308.728 km.

What is the lap record at Circuit of the Americas?

The race lap record is 1:36.169, set by Charles Leclerc (Ferrari) in 2019.

Who was on pole for the 2026 United States Grand Prix?

Kimi Antonelli (Mercedes) took pole with a 1:34.035. On that lap the car reached 341 km/h and averaged 208 km/h.

Who won the 2026 United States Grand Prix?

Kimi Antonelli won the 2026 United States Grand Prix, in a race time of 1:35:16.650.

When was the first Formula 1 race at Circuit of the Americas?

2012.
