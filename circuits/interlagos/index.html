Autódromo José Carlos Pace

São Paulo · Round 20 of the 2026 season · São Paulo Grand Prix 2026

Autódromo José Carlos Pace is a 4.309 km Formula 1 circuit in São Paulo with 15 corners, first used for a world championship Grand Prix in 1973. The 2026 São Paulo Grand Prix is on 8 November 2026. Lando Norris's 2025 pole lap of 1:09.511 peaked at 336 km/h.

Interesting facts

  • "Between lakes". That's what Interlagos means; the circuit sits between two reservoirs in São Paulo.
  • Anti-clockwise, one of the few tracks on the calendar to run that way, which tests drivers' necks.
  • Named after José Carlos Pace, the Brazilian driver who won here in 1975.
  • A natural bowl. The lap rises and falls 42.6 m, from the Senna S down to the Juncão and back up the hill.

Questions and answers

How long is Autódromo José Carlos Pace?

Autódromo José Carlos Pace is 4.309 km long, with 15 corners. The 2026 São Paulo Grand Prix runs over 71 laps, a race distance of 305.939 km.

What is the lap record at Autódromo José Carlos Pace?

The race lap record is 1:10.540, set by Valtteri Bottas (Mercedes) in 2018.

Who was on pole for the 2025 São Paulo Grand Prix?

Lando Norris (McLaren) took pole with a 1:09.511. On that lap the car reached 336 km/h and averaged 219 km/h.

When is the 2026 São Paulo Grand Prix?

Round 20 of 23, on 8 November 2026 (6–8 Nov). The race starts at 17:00 UTC.

When was the first Formula 1 race at Autódromo José Carlos Pace?

1973.
