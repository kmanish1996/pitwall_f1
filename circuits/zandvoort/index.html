Circuit Zandvoort

Zandvoort · Round 12 of the 2026 season · Dutch Grand Prix 2026

Circuit Zandvoort is a 4.259 km Formula 1 circuit in Zandvoort with 14 corners, first used for a world championship Grand Prix in 1952. Lando Norris won the 2026 Dutch Grand Prix here. Lando Norris's 2026 pole lap of 1:11.163 peaked at 316 km/h.

Interesting facts

  • In the dunes. The circuit winds through the sand dunes just behind the North Sea beach.
  • Back after 36 years. F1 returned in 2021 after last racing here in 1985.
  • Banked corners. Turn 3 and the final corner are banked at up to 19 degrees, far steeper than most F1 corners.
  • A sea of orange. Packed with Max Verstappen's fans, it's one of the loudest crowds of the season.

Questions and answers

How long is Circuit Zandvoort?

Circuit Zandvoort is 4.259 km long, with 14 corners. The 2026 Dutch Grand Prix runs over 72 laps, a race distance of 306.648 km.

What is the lap record at Circuit Zandvoort?

The race lap record is 1:11.097, set by Lewis Hamilton (Mercedes) in 2021.

Who was on pole for the 2026 Dutch Grand Prix?

Lando Norris (McLaren) took pole with a 1:11.163. On that lap the car reached 316 km/h and averaged 214 km/h.

Who won the 2026 Dutch Grand Prix?

Lando Norris won the 2026 Dutch Grand Prix, in a race time of 2:04:44.859.

When was the first Formula 1 race at Circuit Zandvoort?

1952.
