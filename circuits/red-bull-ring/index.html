Red Bull Ring

Spielberg · Round 8 of the 2026 season · Austrian Grand Prix 2026

Red Bull Ring is a 4.318 km Formula 1 circuit in Spielberg with 10 corners, first used for a world championship Grand Prix in 1970. George Russell won the 2026 Austrian Grand Prix here. George Russell's 2026 pole lap of 1:06.113 peaked at 329 km/h.

Interesting facts

  • One lap, about a minute. With just 10 corners, the pole lap took barely over a minute, one of the shortest laps of the year.
  • Up the mountain and back. Set in the hills of Styria, the lap climbs and falls 63.2 m.
  • Three names, one place. It was the Österreichring from 1970, the shorter A1-Ring from 1997, and the Red Bull Ring since F1 returned in 2014.

Questions and answers

How long is Red Bull Ring?

Red Bull Ring is 4.318 km long, with 10 corners. The 2026 Austrian Grand Prix runs over 71 laps, a race distance of 306.578 km.

What is the lap record at Red Bull Ring?

The race lap record is 1:05.619, set by Carlos Sainz (McLaren) in 2020.

Who was on pole for the 2026 Austrian Grand Prix?

George Russell (Mercedes) took pole with a 1:06.113. On that lap the car reached 329 km/h and averaged 234 km/h.

Who won the 2026 Austrian Grand Prix?

George Russell won the 2026 Austrian Grand Prix, in a race time of 1:26:37.979.

When was the first Formula 1 race at Red Bull Ring?

1970.
