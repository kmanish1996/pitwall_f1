Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez

Mexico City · Round 19 of the 2026 season · Mexico City Grand Prix 2026

Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez is a 4.304 km Formula 1 circuit in Mexico City with 17 corners, first used for a world championship Grand Prix in 1963. Max Verstappen won the 2026 Mexico City Grand Prix here. Lewis Hamilton's 2026 pole lap of 1:16.514 peaked at 350 km/h.

Interesting facts

  • Thin air. At about 2,200 m above sea level, there's less drag and less downforce. The pole lap hit 350 km/h, the highest top speed of any track here.
  • Through a stadium. The lap passes through the Foro Sol, a former baseball stadium, in front of a huge grandstand.
  • Named after two brothers, Ricardo and Pedro Rodríguez, Mexico's racing heroes of the 1960s.
  • Engines and brakes struggle to stay cool in the thin air, so teams open up their cooling.

Questions and answers

How long is Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez?

Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez is 4.304 km long, with 17 corners. The 2026 Mexico City Grand Prix runs over 71 laps, a race distance of 305.584 km.

What is the lap record at Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez?

The race lap record is 1:17.774, set by Valtteri Bottas (Mercedes) in 2021.

Who was on pole for the 2026 Mexico City Grand Prix?

Lewis Hamilton (Ferrari) took pole with a 1:16.514. On that lap the car reached 350 km/h and averaged 200 km/h.

Who won the 2026 Mexico City Grand Prix?

Max Verstappen won the 2026 Mexico City Grand Prix, in a race time of 2:20:28.772.

When was the first Formula 1 race at Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez?

1963.
