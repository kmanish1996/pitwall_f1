Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps

Stavelot · Round 10 of the 2026 season · Belgian Grand Prix 2026

Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps is a 7.004 km Formula 1 circuit in Stavelot with 20 corners, first used for a world championship Grand Prix in 1950. Kimi Antonelli won the 2026 Belgian Grand Prix here. Kimi Antonelli's 2026 pole lap of 1:44.361 peaked at 336 km/h.

Interesting facts

  • The longest lap of the year, at just over 7 km through the forests of the Ardennes.
  • Eau Rouge and Raidillon. Cars plunge into a dip and then climb steeply uphill through this left-right-left, almost flat out.
  • Rain on one side, sun on the other. The lap is so long that it can be raining on one part of the track and dry on another.
  • A real roller coaster. The pole lap covers 102.0 m between its lowest and highest points, the most of any track here.
  • Flat out for 69% of the lap on the pole lap.

Questions and answers

How long is Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps?

Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps is 7.004 km long, with 20 corners. The 2026 Belgian Grand Prix runs over 44 laps, a race distance of 308.176 km.

What is the lap record at Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps?

The race lap record is 1:44.701, set by Sergio Pérez (Red Bull) in 2024.

Who was on pole for the 2026 Belgian Grand Prix?

Kimi Antonelli (Mercedes) took pole with a 1:44.361. On that lap the car reached 336 km/h and averaged 240 km/h.

Who won the 2026 Belgian Grand Prix?

Kimi Antonelli won the 2026 Belgian Grand Prix, in a race time of 1:24:42.479.

When was the first Formula 1 race at Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps?

1950.
