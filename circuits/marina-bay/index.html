Marina Bay Street Circuit

Singapore · Round 17 of the 2026 season · Singapore Grand Prix 2026

Marina Bay Street Circuit is a 4.940 km Formula 1 circuit in Singapore with 19 corners, first used for a world championship Grand Prix in 2008. Max Verstappen won the 2026 Singapore Grand Prix here. Max Verstappen's 2026 pole lap of 1:31.373 peaked at 308 km/h.

Interesting facts

  • F1's first night race, held in 2008 under floodlights through the city centre.
  • Brutal on drivers. Heat, humidity and a long race make it one of the most physically demanding of the year.
  • A new layout in 2023. The tight section under a grandstand at the old Turns 16–19 was replaced by a straight, shortening the lap.
  • Corner after corner. Max Verstappen's pole lap used 58 gear changes.

Questions and answers

How long is Marina Bay Street Circuit?

Marina Bay Street Circuit is 4.940 km long, with 19 corners. The 2026 Singapore Grand Prix runs over 62 laps, a race distance of 306.280 km.

What is the lap record at Marina Bay Street Circuit?

The race lap record is 1:33.808, set by Lewis Hamilton (Ferrari) in 2025.

Who was on pole for the 2026 Singapore Grand Prix?

Max Verstappen (Red Bull Racing) took pole with a 1:31.373. On that lap the car reached 308 km/h and averaged 192 km/h.

Who won the 2026 Singapore Grand Prix?

Max Verstappen won the 2026 Singapore Grand Prix, in a race time of 1:42:00.785.

When was the first Formula 1 race at Marina Bay Street Circuit?

2008.
