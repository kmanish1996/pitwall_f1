Lusail International Circuit

Lusail · Round 22 of the 2026 season · Qatar Grand Prix 2026

Lusail International Circuit is a 5.419 km Formula 1 circuit in Lusail with 16 corners, first used for a world championship Grand Prix in 2021. The 2026 Qatar Grand Prix is on 29 November 2026. Oscar Piastri's 2025 pole lap of 1:19.387 peaked at 327 km/h.

Interesting facts

  • Built for motorbikes. The circuit opened in 2004 for MotoGP and first hosted F1 in 2021.
  • A floodlit night race in the desert north of Doha.
  • Fast, flowing corners one after another make it very hard on tyres and on drivers' necks.
  • Hardly any slow corners. The slowest point of the pole lap was 99 km/h.

Questions and answers

How long is Lusail International Circuit?

Lusail International Circuit is 5.419 km long, with 16 corners. The 2026 Qatar Grand Prix runs over 57 laps, a race distance of 308.883 km.

What is the lap record at Lusail International Circuit?

The race lap record is 1:22.384, set by Lando Norris (McLaren) in 2024.

Who was on pole for the 2025 Qatar Grand Prix?

Oscar Piastri (McLaren) took pole with a 1:19.387. On that lap the car reached 327 km/h and averaged 245 km/h.

When is the 2026 Qatar Grand Prix?

Round 22 of 23, on 29 November 2026 (27–29 Nov). The race starts at 16:00 UTC.

When was the first Formula 1 race at Lusail International Circuit?

2021.
