Silverstone Circuit

Silverstone · Round 9 of the 2026 season · British Grand Prix 2026

Silverstone Circuit is a 5.891 km Formula 1 circuit in Silverstone with 18 corners, first used for a world championship Grand Prix in 1950. Charles Leclerc won the 2026 British Grand Prix here. Kimi Antonelli's 2026 pole lap of 1:28.111 peaked at 317 km/h.

Interesting facts

  • Where it all began. Silverstone hosted the first ever Formula 1 World Championship race, on 13 May 1950.
  • An old airfield. The track was built on a Second World War RAF bomber base, and the first layouts used its runways.
  • Maggotts, Becketts and Chapel. This flick left-right-left-right is one of the fastest series of direction changes in F1.
  • Fast and flowing. Most of the lap is at high speed: the slowest point of the pole lap was 87 km/h.

Questions and answers

How long is Silverstone Circuit?

Silverstone Circuit is 5.891 km long, with 18 corners. The 2026 British Grand Prix runs over 52 laps, a race distance of 306.332 km.

What is the lap record at Silverstone Circuit?

The race lap record is 1:27.097, set by Max Verstappen (Red Bull) in 2020.

Who was on pole for the 2026 British Grand Prix?

Kimi Antonelli (Mercedes) took pole with a 1:28.111. On that lap the car reached 317 km/h and averaged 238 km/h.

Who won the 2026 British Grand Prix?

Charles Leclerc won the 2026 British Grand Prix, in a race time of 1:27:11.335.

When was the first Formula 1 race at Silverstone Circuit?

1950.
