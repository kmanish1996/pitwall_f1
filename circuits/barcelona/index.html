Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya

Montmeló · Round 7 of the 2026 season · Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix 2026

Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya is a 4.657 km Formula 1 circuit in Montmeló with 14 corners, first used for a world championship Grand Prix in 1991. Lewis Hamilton won the 2026 Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix here. George Russell's 2026 pole lap of 1:14.679 peaked at 341 km/h.

Interesting facts

  • Opened in 1991, in time for the Barcelona Olympics the following year.
  • Teams know every bump. It has long been a favourite pre-season testing venue, so car set-ups here are rarely a surprise.
  • The final chicane is gone. The slow chicane before the last corner was removed in 2023, bringing back the fast sweep onto the main straight.
  • Now its own Grand Prix. With the Spanish Grand Prix moving to Madrid in 2026, Barcelona races as the Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix.

Questions and answers

How long is Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya?

Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya is 4.657 km long, with 14 corners. The 2026 Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix runs over 66 laps, a race distance of 307.362 km.

What is the lap record at Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya?

The race lap record is 1:15.743, set by Oscar Piastri (McLaren) in 2025.

Who was on pole for the 2026 Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix?

George Russell (Mercedes) took pole with a 1:14.679. On that lap the car reached 341 km/h and averaged 223 km/h.

Who won the 2026 Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix?

Lewis Hamilton won the 2026 Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix, in a race time of 1:32:28.105.

When was the first Formula 1 race at Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya?

1991.
