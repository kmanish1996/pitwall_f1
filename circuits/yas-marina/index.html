Yas Marina Circuit

Abu Dhabi · Round 23 of the 2026 season · Abu Dhabi Grand Prix 2026

Yas Marina Circuit is a 5.281 km Formula 1 circuit in Abu Dhabi with 16 corners, first used for a world championship Grand Prix in 2009. The 2026 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix is on 6 December 2026. Max Verstappen's 2025 pole lap of 1:22.207 peaked at 331 km/h.

Interesting facts

  • Day into night. The race starts in daylight and finishes under floodlights.
  • A tunnel for the pit lane. The pit exit dives under the track before rejoining.
  • The finale. It has hosted the last race of the season most years, including the 2021 title decider between Max Verstappen and Lewis Hamilton.
  • Redesigned in 2021 to remove slow corners and give more chances to overtake.

Questions and answers

How long is Yas Marina Circuit?

Yas Marina Circuit is 5.281 km long, with 16 corners. The 2026 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix runs over 58 laps, a race distance of 306.298 km.

What is the lap record at Yas Marina Circuit?

The race lap record is 1:25.637, set by Kevin Magnussen (Haas) in 2024.

Who was on pole for the 2025 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix?

Max Verstappen (Red Bull Racing) took pole with a 1:22.207. On that lap the car reached 331 km/h and averaged 230 km/h.

When is the 2026 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix?

Round 23 of 23, on 6 December 2026 (4–6 Dec). The race starts at 13:00 UTC.

When was the first Formula 1 race at Yas Marina Circuit?

2009.
