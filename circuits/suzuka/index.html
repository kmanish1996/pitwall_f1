Suzuka International Racing Course

Suzuka · Round 3 of the 2026 season · Japanese Grand Prix 2026

Suzuka International Racing Course is a 5.807 km Formula 1 circuit in Suzuka with 18 corners, first used for a world championship Grand Prix in 1987. Kimi Antonelli won the 2026 Japanese Grand Prix here. Kimi Antonelli's 2026 pole lap of 1:28.778 peaked at 321 km/h.

Interesting facts

  • A figure of eight. It's the only track on the calendar that crosses over itself, with the back straight running over a bridge above the first part of the lap.
  • Built by Honda in 1962 as a test track, and still owned by a Honda company.
  • The S Curves and 130R. The flowing first sector and the near-flat-out 130R left-hander are among the most respected corners in racing.
  • Where titles are won. Suzuka has settled many world championships, including the Senna–Prost collisions of 1989 and 1990.
  • Real hills. The pole lap climbs and falls 40.1 m between its lowest and highest points.

Questions and answers

How long is Suzuka International Racing Course?

Suzuka International Racing Course is 5.807 km long, with 18 corners. The 2026 Japanese Grand Prix runs over 53 laps, a race distance of 307.771 km.

What is the lap record at Suzuka International Racing Course?

The race lap record is 1:30.965, set by Kimi Antonelli (Mercedes) in 2025.

Who was on pole for the 2026 Japanese Grand Prix?

Kimi Antonelli (Mercedes) took pole with a 1:28.778. On that lap the car reached 321 km/h and averaged 234 km/h.

Who won the 2026 Japanese Grand Prix?

Kimi Antonelli won the 2026 Japanese Grand Prix, in a race time of 1:28:03.403.

When was the first Formula 1 race at Suzuka International Racing Course?

1987.
