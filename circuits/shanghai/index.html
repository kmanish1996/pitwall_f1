Shanghai International Circuit

Shanghai · Round 2 of the 2026 season · Chinese Grand Prix 2026

Shanghai International Circuit is a 5.451 km Formula 1 circuit in Shanghai with 16 corners, first used for a world championship Grand Prix in 2004. Kimi Antonelli won the 2026 Chinese Grand Prix here. Kimi Antonelli's 2026 pole lap of 1:32.064 peaked at 332 km/h.

Interesting facts

  • Shaped like a word. The layout follows the Chinese character 上 (shàng), meaning "above" or "rise", the first character of Shanghai's name.
  • A first corner that never seems to end. Turns 1 and 2 keep tightening as they curl back on themselves, so drivers brake and turn at the same time for a long way.
  • Built on marshland. The track sits on a foundation of polystyrene blocks to stop it sinking into the soft ground.
  • A huge back straight of around 1.2 km into the Turn 14 hairpin, the best overtaking spot on the lap.

Questions and answers

How long is Shanghai International Circuit?

Shanghai International Circuit is 5.451 km long, with 16 corners. The 2026 Chinese Grand Prix runs over 56 laps, a race distance of 305.256 km.

What is the lap record at Shanghai International Circuit?

The race lap record is 1:32.238, set by Michael Schumacher (Ferrari) in 2004.

Who was on pole for the 2026 Chinese Grand Prix?

Kimi Antonelli (Mercedes) took pole with a 1:32.064. On that lap the car reached 332 km/h and averaged 215 km/h.

Who won the 2026 Chinese Grand Prix?

Kimi Antonelli won the 2026 Chinese Grand Prix, in a race time of 1:33:15.607.

When was the first Formula 1 race at Shanghai International Circuit?

2004.
