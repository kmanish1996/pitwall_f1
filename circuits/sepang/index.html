Sepang International Circuit

Sepang · Round 16 of the 2026 season · Bahrain Grand Prix 2026

Sepang International Circuit is a 5.543 km Formula 1 circuit in Sepang with 15 corners, first used for a world championship Grand Prix in 1999. Max Verstappen won the 2026 Bahrain Grand Prix here. Max Verstappen's 2026 pole lap of 1:35.130 peaked at 321 km/h.

Interesting facts

  • The first of a new generation. Opened in 1999, it was the first F1 track designed by Hermann Tilke, who went on to design many more.
  • Home of the Malaysian Grand Prix from 1999 to 2017.
  • Heat, humidity and sudden storms. Tropical downpours can soak the track within minutes.
  • Two long straights meet at the tight final hairpin, side by side in front of the main grandstand.

Questions and answers

How long is Sepang International Circuit?

Sepang International Circuit is 5.543 km long, with 15 corners. The 2026 Bahrain Grand Prix runs over 56 laps, a race distance of 310.408 km.

What is the lap record at Sepang International Circuit?

The race lap record is 1:34.223, set by Juan Pablo Montoya (Williams) in 2004.

Who was on pole for the 2026 Bahrain Grand Prix?

Max Verstappen (Red Bull Racing) took pole with a 1:35.130. On that lap the car reached 321 km/h and averaged 208 km/h.

Who won the 2026 Bahrain Grand Prix?

Max Verstappen won the 2026 Bahrain Grand Prix, in a race time of 1:47:14.808.

When was the first Formula 1 race at Sepang International Circuit?

1999.
