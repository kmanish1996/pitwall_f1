Las Vegas Strip Circuit

Las Vegas · Round 21 of the 2026 season · Las Vegas Grand Prix 2026

Las Vegas Strip Circuit is a 6.201 km Formula 1 circuit in Las Vegas with 17 corners, first used for a world championship Grand Prix in 2023. The 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix is on 22 November 2026. Lando Norris's 2025 pole lap of 1:47.934 peaked at 331 km/h.

Interesting facts

  • Down the Strip at night. Cars race on public roads, past the casinos of the Las Vegas Strip.
  • Not Vegas's first F1 race. In 1981 and 1982, the Caesars Palace Grand Prix was held in a hotel car park.
  • A very long lap, second only to Spa, with a long flat-out run down the Strip.
  • Wet in 2025. This lap is Lando Norris's 2025 pole, set on a wet track, so it's slower than a dry lap would be.

Questions and answers

How long is Las Vegas Strip Circuit?

Las Vegas Strip Circuit is 6.201 km long, with 17 corners. The 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix runs over 50 laps, a race distance of 310.050 km.

What is the lap record at Las Vegas Strip Circuit?

The race lap record is 1:33.365, set by Max Verstappen (Red Bull) in 2025.

Who was on pole for the 2025 Las Vegas Grand Prix?

Lando Norris (McLaren) took pole with a 1:47.934. On that lap the car reached 331 km/h and averaged 206 km/h.

When is the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix?

Round 21 of 23, on 22 November 2026 (19–21 Nov). The race starts at 04:00 UTC.

When was the first Formula 1 race at Las Vegas Strip Circuit?

2023.
