Miami International Autodrome

Miami Gardens · Round 4 of the 2026 season · Miami Grand Prix 2026

Miami International Autodrome is a 5.412 km Formula 1 circuit in Miami Gardens with 19 corners, first used for a world championship Grand Prix in 2022. Kimi Antonelli won the 2026 Miami Grand Prix here. Kimi Antonelli's 2026 pole lap of 1:27.798 peaked at 343 km/h.

Interesting facts

  • Built around a stadium. The track winds around Hard Rock Stadium, home of the NFL's Miami Dolphins, in Miami Gardens.
  • The famous fake marina. The yachts in the "marina" sit on a painted deck with no water underneath.
  • A tight, twisty chicane at Turns 14–15 sits beside very fast sections, so the cars need to be good at both.
  • Flat out for top speed. The pole lap peaked at 343 km/h on the long back straight.

Questions and answers

How long is Miami International Autodrome?

Miami International Autodrome is 5.412 km long, with 19 corners. The 2026 Miami Grand Prix runs over 57 laps, a race distance of 308.484 km.

What is the lap record at Miami International Autodrome?

The race lap record is 1:29.708, set by Max Verstappen (Red Bull) in 2023.

Who was on pole for the 2026 Miami Grand Prix?

Kimi Antonelli (Mercedes) took pole with a 1:27.798. On that lap the car reached 343 km/h and averaged 220 km/h.

Who won the 2026 Miami Grand Prix?

Kimi Antonelli won the 2026 Miami Grand Prix, in a race time of 1:33:19.273.

When was the first Formula 1 race at Miami International Autodrome?

2022.
