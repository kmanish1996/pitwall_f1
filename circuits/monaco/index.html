Circuit de Monaco

Monte Carlo · Round 6 of the 2026 season · Monaco Grand Prix 2026

Circuit de Monaco is a 3.337 km Formula 1 circuit in Monte Carlo with 19 corners, first used for a world championship Grand Prix in 1950. Kimi Antonelli won the 2026 Monaco Grand Prix here. Kimi Antonelli's 2026 pole lap of 1:12.051 peaked at 289 km/h.

Interesting facts

  • The shortest and slowest lap of the season, squeezed between the barriers on the streets of Monte Carlo.
  • The slowest corner in F1. The Fairmont hairpin dropped the pole lap to just 44 km/h.
  • A tunnel on the track. Cars race through the tunnel under the Fairmont hotel, the only one on the calendar, flat out from darkness into daylight.
  • On the calendar since 1950, the first season of the world championship.
  • Up and down the hill. From the harbour up to Casino Square and back, the lap covers 41.2 m of height.

Questions and answers

How long is Circuit de Monaco?

Circuit de Monaco is 3.337 km long, with 19 corners. The 2026 Monaco Grand Prix runs over 78 laps, a race distance of 260.286 km.

What is the lap record at Circuit de Monaco?

The race lap record is 1:12.909, set by Lewis Hamilton (Mercedes) in 2021.

Who was on pole for the 2026 Monaco Grand Prix?

Kimi Antonelli (Mercedes) took pole with a 1:12.051. On that lap the car reached 289 km/h and averaged 164 km/h.

Who won the 2026 Monaco Grand Prix?

Kimi Antonelli won the 2026 Monaco Grand Prix, in a race time of 2:23:31.243.

When was the first Formula 1 race at Circuit de Monaco?

1950.
