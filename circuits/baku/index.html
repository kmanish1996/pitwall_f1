Baku City Circuit

Baku · Round 15 of the 2026 season · Azerbaijan Grand Prix 2026

Baku City Circuit is a 6.003 km Formula 1 circuit in Baku with 20 corners, first used for a world championship Grand Prix in 2016. George Russell won the 2026 Azerbaijan Grand Prix here. George Russell's 2026 pole lap of 1:42.526 peaked at 331 km/h.

Interesting facts

  • Two tracks in one. A flat-out run of about 2.2 km along the seafront meets a tight, twisty section past the old city walls.
  • The castle section. At its narrowest, near Turn 8, the track is only about 7.6 m wide.
  • First race in 2016, when it hosted the European Grand Prix, before becoming the Azerbaijan Grand Prix in 2017.
  • Top speed. The pole lap hit 331 km/h on the long run to the finish line.

Questions and answers

How long is Baku City Circuit?

Baku City Circuit is 6.003 km long, with 20 corners. The 2026 Azerbaijan Grand Prix runs over 51 laps, a race distance of 306.153 km.

What is the lap record at Baku City Circuit?

The race lap record is 1:43.009, set by Charles Leclerc (Ferrari) in 2019.

Who was on pole for the 2026 Azerbaijan Grand Prix?

George Russell (Mercedes) took pole with a 1:42.526. On that lap the car reached 331 km/h and averaged 210 km/h.

Who won the 2026 Azerbaijan Grand Prix?

George Russell won the 2026 Azerbaijan Grand Prix, in a race time of 1:38:02.143.

When was the first Formula 1 race at Baku City Circuit?

2016.
