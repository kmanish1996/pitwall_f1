F1 calendar 2027

All 24 rounds of the 2027 Formula 1 season · official calendar, announced 16 September 2026

The 2027 season starts with pre-season testing in Bahrain (24–27 Feb) and the Bahrain Grand Prix on 14 March, and finishes in Abu Dhabi on 12 December. There are 24 races and a record 10 sprint weekends, including Monaco for the first time. The Portuguese and Turkish Grands Prix return, at Algarve International Circuit and Istanbul Park; the Dutch GP and the Barcelona race drop off.

Questions and answers

When does the 2027 F1 season start?

Pre-season testing runs in Bahrain from 24–27 Feb. The first race is the Bahrain Grand Prix on 14 March 2027, and the season ends at the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix on 12 December.

How many races are there in 2027?

24 Grands Prix, 10 of them sprint weekends.

Which 2027 races have a sprint?

Bahrain, Australian, Japanese, Canadian, Monaco, British, Italian, São Paulo, Qatar, Abu Dhabi.

Which races are new or back in 2027?

the Portuguese Grand Prix (Algarve International Circuit) and the Turkish Grand Prix (Istanbul Park) return. The Dutch Grand Prix is gone: Zandvoort's contract ended after 2026. The Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix is gone: Spain keeps one race, at the Madring in Madrid.
