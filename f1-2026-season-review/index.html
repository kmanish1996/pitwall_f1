How Kimi Antonelli won the 2026 F1 title

The 2026 Formula 1 season, told through the numbers · updated after round 21 of 23

Kimi Antonelli is the 2026 Formula 1 World Champion. He sealed the title at the Mexico City Grand Prix (round 19), when his lead became bigger than the 100 points still available, and he did it with 4 rounds to spare. At 20, he is the youngest world champion in Formula 1 history.

The title race, round by round

How he did it

  • Winning often. 9 Grand Prix wins from 21 rounds, starting at the Chinese Grand Prix, including 5 in a row.
  • Scoring nearly every weekend. 19 points finishes and 16 podiums in 21 rounds, with 1 retirement.
  • Starting at the front. An average grid slot of P3.2 and 8 pole positions.
  • Leading the table. Top of the championship after 19 of 21 rounds, and every round since the Japanese Grand Prix.
  • The nearest challenger: Max Verstappen, 111 points behind after round 21.

Standings after round 21

6 different drivers won Grands Prix so far, for 4 teams: Mercedes 12, Red Bull Racing 4, McLaren 3, Ferrari 2. Mercedes won the constructors' championship with 652 points, 142 ahead of Ferrari.
