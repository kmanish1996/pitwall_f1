Formula 1 world champions

Every World Drivers' Champion from 1950 to 2026.

Kimi Antonelli is the 2026 Formula 1 World Champion with Mercedes. 36 different drivers have won the title since the first championship in 1950. The most successful are Michael Schumacher (7), Lewis Hamilton (7), Juan Manuel Fangio (5), Alain Prost (4).
